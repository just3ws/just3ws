# Mike Hall

**Staff Software Engineer** focused on making complex production systems easier to change, operate, and understand. I work across legacy modernization, observability, distributed systems, and human-led AI-assisted tooling.

## Focus

- **Modernization:** Find safe seams in legacy systems, instrument current behavior, and make change safer through migrations, contracts, and reversible steps.
- **Observability:** Use OpenTelemetry, tracing, service-level objectives, and system maps to connect technical behavior to business outcomes.
- **Panoramic View:** Map workflows from entry to outcome, correlate checkpoints, and compare runs so delays, retries, and exit paths are visible.
- **Bounded AI:** Use local or controlled AI tools for search, transcription, and analysis. Keep provenance, automated checks, and human judgment in the loop.
